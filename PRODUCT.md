## Users

Three overlapping audiences for The Washboard's website, all of whom may be on a phone in the parking lot deciding whether to come in:

- **Murray State University students** — often doing laundry on their own for the first time, anxious about cost, machine operation, and whether the place feels safe. Need clear how-to, price expectations, and reassurance.
- **Working families and locals in Murray / Calloway County** — convenience-driven. Need to know hours, where it is, what it costs, and whether Wash & Fold is available. Many are repeat customers; the site mainly has to make first-timers comfortable and confirm details for everyone else.
- **Apartment residents** — renters in town without in-unit laundry. Weekly visitors who care about a clean, comfortable, well-lit space they can spend an hour in without dreading it.
- **Out-of-town visitors** — especially travelers near Kentucky Lake who need a laundromat while on a trip. They find the site via "laundromat near me" / Google Maps and decide in 15 seconds whether to drive over. Photos, hours, and address clarity drive the visit.

The job to be done across all four: **decide whether to come in today, and arrive without anxiety about how it works.**

### Personas

**Sarah, Murray State student (19–22).** Lives in a dorm without in-unit laundry. Has $20 to spend on laundry and 90 minutes between classes. Worried about: not knowing how the card system works, ending up somewhere that feels sketchy at night, breaking a machine. Wins if: she finds clear instructions before she arrives, knows the price, and feels the place is well-lit and safe.

**Jennifer, busy parent (30–45).** Home washer is broken or she's behind on family laundry. Looking for Wash & Fold so she can drop off and go. Worried about: whether her laundry will come back clean and on time, and whether the price is honest. Wins if: she sees clear Wash & Fold pricing, attendant hours, and reads a few real reviews that mention the staff.

**Mike, apartment resident (25–50).** Weekly laundry routine. Mainly cares about a clean, modern, coin-free facility close to home. Worried about: dingy machines, broken dryers, no parking. Wins if: photos and reviews show the place is well-maintained, hours are wide, and the card system is easy.

## Product Purpose

A marketing site for The Washboard, an independently owned laundromat at 605 S. 12th St in Murray, KY. The site exists to attract first-time visitors, reassure repeat customers, and answer the practical questions (hours, pricing, location, how the cards work, Wash & Fold) that make someone walk through the door instead of driving somewhere else.

Success looks like: a stranger lands on the site from search, finds what they need within two interactions (hours, address, "how does it work"), feels the place is clean and welcoming, and goes.

### Business Goals

1. Rank top-3 for "laundromat Murray KY" and adjacent local searches.
2. Drive foot traffic to 605 S. 12th St, Murray, KY 42071.
3. Promote Wash & Fold as a time-saving service for busy families and students.
4. Build trust through visible Google reviews and a community presence.
5. Educate first-time visitors on the card system and machine usage so the first visit feels easy.

## Brand Personality

**Bright, welcoming, dependable** — with a neighborly touch and a modern, efficient backbone.

The voice should sound like a real person who works at the laundromat, not a chain's marketing team. First-person plural ("we", "our") is fine. Warm and friendly without being twee or overly folksy. Modern enough that students don't think the place is dated; warm enough that older locals feel comfortable.

Emotional goals: confidence ("this place will be clean and the machines will work"), ease ("I won't be embarrassed if I don't know what I'm doing"), welcome ("this feels like a nice place to spend an hour").

### Writing Style

- Short paragraphs (2–4 sentences). Active voice. Plain language.
- Clear headings; bullet points where they earn their keep.
- Location keywords (Murray, Calloway County, Murray State) appear naturally, never stuffed.
- Primary CTA: "Get Directions." Secondary CTAs: "Call 270-768-7058", "View Services", "Read More".
- Action-oriented but never pushy. No salesy language, no exclamation marks chained together, no "Don't miss out!" energy.

## Anti-references

The site should explicitly NOT look or feel like:

- **Cold corporate chain** (Tide Cleaners, big-box franchise laundromats). Sterile stock photos, generic "your one-stop laundry solution" copy, no sense of a real place.
- **Trendy SaaS startup**. Gradients, dark mode, hero-metric templates, "AI-powered", neon accents. Wrong register for a physical small business.
- **Generic small-business template**. Cookie-cutter Wix/GoDaddy aesthetic, same hero, same three icon boxes, same testimonial slider that every plumber/dentist/laundromat site uses.
- **Run-down old laundromat**. Handwritten signs, fluorescent vibe, dingy textures. Visually conveys "not maintained."

## Design Principles

1. **Show, don't tell.** "Clean and modern" is a claim. Photos of the actual space, accurate hours, real prices, and clear instructions are proof. Always prefer proof over adjectives.
2. **Sound like a person, not a chain.** First-person voice, plain language, neighborly tone. If a sentence sounds like it could appear on any laundromat's website, rewrite it so it could only appear on ours.
3. **Concrete over generic.** Real numbers ($3.50–$7.50), real ledges ("above the green LCD screen"), real names of products (Tide, Gain), real neighborhoods (Murray State, Calloway County). Specificity is the antidote to template feel.
4. **Easy first visit.** Every page should be readable by someone who has never set foot in the building. No assumed knowledge. Card system, hours, location, what to bring, all surfaced clearly.
5. **Earn trust through clarity.** Clear pricing, clear hours, clear how-to. Ambiguity reads as carelessness for a service business; precision reads as competence.
6. **Mobile-first, parking-lot-ready.** A lot of decisions happen on a phone in a car. Hours, address, and the click-to-call button are never more than a thumb-tap away.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target **WCAG 2.1 AA**:

- 4.5:1 contrast on body text, 3:1 on large text and UI components.
- Keyboard navigation works on every interactive element; visible focus states.
- Alt text on all content imagery; decorative images marked appropriately.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Touch targets ≥44×44px (a lot of visits are on phones in the parking lot).
- Body type at a comfortable default size (currently 17px), kept generous for older locals.
- Semantic HTML with a proper heading hierarchy; ARIA labels only where genuinely needed.

### Long-tail keywords
- laundromat near Murray State University
- best laundromat Murray KY
- laundromat Murray KY hours
- coin laundry Calloway County
- "open now" / "when are you open" queries (handle these on Contact and homepage hours section)

### Structured data requirements
- LocalBusiness / Laundromat JSON-LD on the homepage and contact page (already in `BaseLayout.astro` and `contact.astro`).
- FAQPage schema on the How-To page.
- Service schema for each named offering.

### Content strategy notes
- Blog topics rotate across seasonal (Spring Cleaning, Back to School, Bulky Winter Bedding), service education (Wash & Fold value, specialty items), student content (Dorm laundry tips), local community, and practical guides.
- Keep posts under ~1,200 words. Each post links back to the relevant `/services` or `/how-to` page.
- Avoid keyword stuffing. Mention Murray naturally in headlines, intros, and image alt text.

## Competitive Context

**iWash Murray** (1550 Lowes Drive, also a Benton location) is the main competitor. Open 24/7. Also offers Wash & Fold.

The Washboard differentiates on:
- Cleaner, more modern facility (reflected in 4.9-star Google rating).
- Better customer service (a recurring theme in reviews).
- Stronger online presence and clearer information architecture.
- Community-rooted, locally focused brand voice — not a chain.

## Success Metrics

1. **SEO** — Top-3 ranking for "laundromat Murray KY"; page-1 for 10+ local long-tail queries.
2. **Traffic** — 500–1,000 organic visits / month; 50+ click-to-call interactions; 100+ directions requests.
3. **Conversion** — Increase Wash & Fold inquiries; lift foot traffic during 9 AM–3 PM slow hours.
4. **Engagement** — Blog post views as a proxy for trust; review-card click-through; mobile menu contact interactions.

## Technical Notes

- **Framework:** Astro (static site generation).
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS v4 with custom design tokens in `src/index.css`.
- **Fonts:** Oswald (headings, navigation, buttons), Source Sans 3 (body).
- **Hosting:** Vercel with automatic GitHub deployments.
- **JS budget:** Effectively zero. Mobile menu toggle is the only client-side interactivity.
- **Performance targets:** FCP < 1.5s, LCP < 2.5s, TTI < 3.5s, CLS < 0.1.
